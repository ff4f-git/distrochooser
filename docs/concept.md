# Concept

**[Deutsch](konzept.md)**

**There is no single best Linux distribution. It depends on your needs.**

The Distrochooser helps beginners and people switching to Linux find a suitable distribution without technical jargon. Eight clear questions and conditional RAM/GPU follow-ups cover usage, hardware and preferences.

Recommendations use a deliberately **selective**, editorially maintained scoring matrix. Scores reflect a distribution's practical suitability as a starting point, not what could be achieved with unlimited customization.

The top three **distinct** totals define the ranks. Ties may result in more than three recommendations. Bars show relative scores, not guaranteed hardware compatibility.

The questionnaire runs in the browser; the current catalog is fetched from the published catalog service with a static JSON fallback. Optional statistics are submitted only with explicit consent.
